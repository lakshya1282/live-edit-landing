# Direction Card: live-edit (Art Book Proofing Desk Edition)

CONCEPT: This site is an Art Book Proofing Desk that treats live code editing like surgical highlighter & ink annotations on physical gallery plates.
WHY IT FITS THE AUDIENCE: Developers and designers value craft, precision, and physical immediacy; stripping away generic SaaS tropes in favor of an editorial art book proofing table makes the product instantly stand out among hundreds of AI dev tools.
FEELING IN 3 SECONDS: Raw editorial authority, tangible print craft, and surgical speed. High contrast, tactile, unmistakably custom.
SEED: Counter-Print Books / Art Catalog (Awwwards Honorable Mention)
ANCHORS INHERITED:
  - Motion language: Kinetic momentum glide, elastic track reveals, velocity-based skew.
  - Type hierarchy: High-impact editorial display with tight tracking, paired with dense catalog folio labels and marker annotations.
  - Spacing rhythm: Split-screen bilateral layout, asymmetrical registration marks, wide margins.
MUTATIONS APPLIED:
  - Background: Two-tone split screen (deep basalt #0e1013 vs warm stone bone #f4f1ea).
  - Palette: Fluorescent acid lime (#d9ff00) on muted stone neutrals and carbon black ink.
  - Type: Handwritten marker display (`Caveat`) for editorial margin notes + sharp grotesk (`Sora`) + technical monospaced (`Space Mono / Martian Mono`).
  - Layout Skeleton: Horizontal scroll exhibition deck and persistent bilateral split-screen.
  - Motion Signature: Velocity-based skew and stretch tied to scroll speed.
  - Extra Constraint: Rotate at least 3 elements deliberately (-3deg inspection stamp, +2.5deg code sticker, -2deg proof note).
SIGNATURE MOVES:
  - Layout: Bilateral split screen (Dark interactive stage left | Light editorial folio right).
  - Type: Hand-drawn marker scribbles & arrows pointing directly at the in-situ editing controls.
  - Motion: Elastic track scrub and live simulated DOM AST synchronization.
MEMORABLE MOMENTS:
  1. The Split-Screen Living Proof Table.
  2. The Horizontal Art Catalog Plates.
  3. The Rotated Verification Stamp & Marker Annotations.
AVOIDING:
  - Purple/blue gradients, glowing ambient blur blobs.
  - Generic SaaS 3-card pricing/feature grids.
  - Fake timestamps or generic template filler.
